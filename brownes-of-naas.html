Our Story

Family Run

At Brownes Menswear we have been styling the men of Kildare and beyond for over 35 years.

We are a proud member of the Naas community, based on North Main Street right in the heart of town.

Come in, take your time, and let us help you put a proper outfit together, whether its formal, casual, or somewhere in between.
